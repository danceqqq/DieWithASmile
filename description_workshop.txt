[h1]The End Of A Calamity Menu Theme[/h1]
A fan-made tModLoader main menu theme inspired by Calamitas and the song "Die With A Smile".

This mod is [b]client-side[/b] and only changes the title screen. You can keep it enabled in multiplayer without the server or other players needing it. It does not add items, bosses, or gameplay.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Client-side title screen[/b] — custom art, logo, menu styling, and nothing else in the game
[*][b]Collapsible music player[/b] with a reactive spectrum that follows the playing audio, including custom uploads, and freezes when paused
[*][b]Draggable player[/b] — hover to expand, then drag by empty space; the collapsed circle stays with it. The position is saved after leaving a world, restarting, or reloading the mod
[*][b]Playlist[/b] — play, pause, skip, seek, loop one track, or shuffle your enabled songs
[*][b]Custom uploads[/b] — add your own songs from the player; MP3 and WAV convert to OGG with a progress bar. Click a custom track's artist name to rename it (tags are read on upload)
[*][b]Broken folder[/b] — unplayable songs are skipped and moved so the mod still loads
[*][b]Background gallery[/b] — follow the music, lock a scene, or shuffle backgrounds every 0–15 seconds (click scenes to choose the pool; 0 only changes when you leave a world)
[*][b]Logo gallery[/b] — five title-screen logos, including layered hand and sticker variants, plus uploads and other mods' menus
[*][b]Custom art[/b] — PNG/JPEG logos and wallpapers in DieWithASmile/logo and DieWithASmile/wallpaper
[*][b]Other-mod skies[/b] — pick another enabled menu theme's title background (including Wrath of the Gods) or Terraria's default Forest sky; gallery cards use the tModLoader logo or that mod's icon
[*][b]Edit Position[/b] — drag the logo, player, and menu buttons; scroll to resize the logo; pan uploaded wallpapers; Save stores the layout
[*][b]Accent colors[/b] — swatches in the galleries retint the player, hover, buttons, and UI icons (darker idle, lighter on hover)
[*][b]Scene-changing tracks[/b] — Don't Forget fades into a Deltarune-inspired heart scene; Come Along with Me fades into a letter scene with mouse-reactive hands
[*][b]Gallery arts[/b] — Calamitas, Don't Forget, Come Along with Me, Last meeting, Yharim Never Die, Witch of Supreme Memories, and The Soul of the Universe
[*][b]Main Menu Music[/b] volume slider under Settings → Volume
[*][b]Smooth music fades[/b] when mods finish loading, when entering a world, and when returning to the menu
[*][b]Persistence[/b] — selected theme, playlist edits, uploads, loop, shuffle, background choice, volume, and player position are remembered
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Compatible with [b]Sam's Cooler Menu Screen[/b]. If that core theme is selected, the music player still collapses to a circle until you hover it, and can be dragged like in the standard menu (requested by @Mihail04).
