The End Of A Calamity Menu Theme is a fan-made tModLoader main menu theme inspired by Calamitas and the song "Die With A Smile".

It is a client-side title-screen theme: you can keep it enabled in multiplayer without the server or your friends needing it. It replaces the title screen with custom artwork, a music player with a reactive spectrum, logo, and menu styling. Hover the player to expand it, then drag it by empty space — the collapsed circle stays with it. The spectrum follows the playing audio, including custom uploads, and freezes when paused. From the player you can shuffle, loop, edit the playlist, and upload songs; MP3 and WAV are converted to OGG. A gallery beside the logo lets you follow the music, lock a background, or shuffle scenes (including only when leaving a world). A second button under it lets you pick the title-screen logo, upload your own PNG/JPEG, or use another enabled mod's menu art. Edit Position lets you drag the logo, player, and menu buttons and scroll-resize the logo. Menu music has its own slider under Settings → Volume, and fades smoothly when loading, entering a world, or returning to the menu. Playlist edits, uploads, background choice, shuffle, volume, and player position are remembered after restarting the game or reloading the mod. Nothing else in the game is changed.

Features
- Client-side title screen only: custom art, logo, and menu styling
- Collapsible, draggable music player with a reactive spectrum
- Playlist editor, loop, shuffle, seek, and custom uploads (MP3 / WAV to OGG)
- Unplayable songs are skipped and moved to the Broken folder
- Gallery backgrounds: follow music, lock a scene, or shuffle scenes (pick which of the seven cycle; 0s = only when leaving a world)
- Five title-screen logos, including layered hand and sticker variants
- Upload your own logos and backgrounds (PNG / JPEG) or pick art from other enabled menu-theme mods
- Other mods' title skies (including Wrath of the Gods) and Terraria's default Forest sky can be chosen from the gallery
- Edit Position: drag logo, player, and menu buttons; scroll to resize the logo; pan uploaded wallpapers
- Accent color palettes for the player, galleries, buttons, and UI icons
- Scene-changing tracks: Don't Forget and Come Along with Me
- Gallery arts: Calamitas, Don't Forget, Come Along with Me, Last meeting, Yharim Never Die, Witch of Supreme Memories, The Soul of the Universe
- Main Menu Music volume slider
- Smooth music fades on load and when entering or leaving a world
- Compatible with Sam's Cooler Menu Screen
